## Introduction

Twelve weeks ago I started my NCFE Level 3 Certificate in Cyber Security Practice, and this post is a reflection on finishing it - what the course actually covered, what stuck with me, and where I plan to take these skills next.

## What I Covered

Over the course of the programme, I worked through:

- Core cybersecurity fundamentals and terminology
- Network security and infrastructure basics
- Ethical hacking and penetration testing techniques
- Hands-on lab work using SOC and offensive simulation exercises

Each module built on the last, moving from theory into more practical, lab-based work as the weeks went on.

## Key Takeaways

- **Consistency mattered more than intensity.** Showing up for the live sessions and labs every week, even when the material was tough, made a bigger difference than trying to cram.
- **Hands-on practice cemented the theory.** The simulation labs made concepts like threat modelling and network defence click in a way that reading alone never would have.
- **This is a foundation, not a finish line.** Completing the certificate feels less like an end point and more like the base I now get to build on.

## What's Next

With the course complete, my focus shifts to putting these fundamentals into practice: continuing hands-on work through my home lab, TryHackMe and HackTheBox, and looking for ways to bring cybersecurity awareness into whatever role I take on next.

## Conclusion

Completing this course is a solid marker in my transition from law to tech, and proof that the fundamentals I set out to learn back in May have actually landed. I'll keep documenting the next steps here as I take this knowledge further.
