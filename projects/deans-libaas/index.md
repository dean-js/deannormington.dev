## Why I Built This

Working as an e-commerce admin assistant for an Islamic clothing retailer gave me the day-to-day basics, but a live store isn't the place to experiment. Shopify's free development stores solve that, so I set one up, Dean's Libaas, modelled on the same kind of modest/Islamic clothing catalogue as my day job, so the practice actually transfers back.

## What This Covers

- Building out a small catalogue from scratch: Mehaar Purple, Luxe Arielle Saree, Maira Kaftan Pink, Faryal X, and Daisy Co Ord, across full size runs and mixed pricing
- Structuring collections (Home page, Royale, Luxury) rather than just adding products to one flat list
- Getting properly familiar with this year's newer Shopify admin, at my own pace
- Read the full write-up in the [Building Dean's Libaas](/building-deans-libaas-a-shopify-dev-store/) blog post

## Why It Matters

Practising on a store with nothing riding on it means I can push further, try theme changes, restructure collections, test bulk edits, than I'd risk on a live shop. It's already sharpened how I think about catalogue structure and admin workflow in my actual role.

## What's Next

Adding real product photography and full descriptions, customising the "Royale" theme so the storefront reads as a finished concept rather than a placeholder, and using the store to practice discounts, basic reporting, and order workflows once there's a fuller catalogue in place.
