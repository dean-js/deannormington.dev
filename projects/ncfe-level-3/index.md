## What the Certificate Covered

A twelve-week, fully online course delivered through live sessions and a self-paced learning platform. It moved from core theory into hands-on, lab-based work: cybersecurity fundamentals and terminology, network security and infrastructure, ethical hacking and penetration testing techniques, and simulation exercises covering both defensive (SOC-style) and offensive scenarios.

## Key Skills

- Threat modelling and network defence fundamentals
- Planning and implementing security controls across digital infrastructure
- Hands-on practice with SOC simulation and offensive simulation exercises
- Working confidently in a lab environment, not just following theory

## How It Connects to My Other Work

This certificate is the foundation for the growing cyber security side of what I do, running alongside my [Cisco Academy](/projects/cisco-cybersecurity-learning/) study and hands-on [TryHackMe and home lab work](/projects/cybersecurity-projects/). It's a secondary focus to the core web development and Shopify work, but a genuine one I keep building on.
